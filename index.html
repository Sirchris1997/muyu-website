About MUYU

MUYU (木魚) is a community-driven digital asset deployed on BNB Smart Chain. The project is centered on building an accessible and transparent token community around the MUYU brand and its ecosystem activities.


MUYU has a fixed total supply of 20 billion tokens and uses a verified BEP-20 smart contract designed with a simple and transparent structure.
